Meta Tactical

Meta Tactical designs pistol chassis and conversion kits for shooters who want more stability without giving up their favorite handgun. The pistol drops into the chassis with no permanent changes, and it comes back out just as easily.

What the kits do

  • Add a stable shoulder or brace contact point for better follow-up shots
  • Give you full-length rail space for red dots, lights and lasers
  • Fit specific models, so the trigger, mag release and slide stay usable

Learn more

Compatibility charts, install guides and current kits are on the official Meta Tactical site.
